Sunday 1 Nov

Baked orecchiette boscaiola (mushrooms, speck, peas)

Pasta80 minWeekend dinner

Ingredients

Pasta bake
  • Dried orecchiette200 g
  • Mixed mushrooms220 g
  • Speck45 g
  • Tomato passata220 ml (scant 1 cup)
  • Béchamel195 g
  • Green peas55 g
  • Mozzarella110 g
  • Grated Parmesan35 g
  • Garlic1 small clove
  • Onion½ small
  • Olive oil, saltfrom the pantry

Method

  1. Sauce. Soften the finely chopped onion in a little oil. Add the passata, a pinch of salt and a small glass of water. Cover and simmer on medium-low 20–25 min.
  2. Speck. Fry the speck strips until slightly crisp. Set aside.
  3. Mushrooms. Chop the mushrooms small. In the same pan cook the garlic in a little oil, add the mushrooms and cook until soft (no salt yet). Add the peas and a pinch of salt; cook 5 min more.
  4. Pasta. Cook the orecchiette in salted water, draining 5–6 min before the pack time (very al dente).
  5. Mix. Stir the béchamel into the tomato sauce. Add the speck, the mushroom and pea mix and the Parmesan. Toss with the pasta and diced mozzarella.
  6. Bake. Transfer to an oven dish, sprinkle with grated cheese and bake at 200°C for about 20 min (a few minutes under the grill if needed). Rest 10 min.

Recipe is from a blog write-up: confirm it against the reel if anything looks off.

Monday 2 Nov

Miso Roasted Eggplant and Rice with Pak Choi, Baby Corn and Pickled Radish

Vegetarian40 min

Ingredients

Eggplant
  • Long eggplant630 g
  • Brown sugar10 g (2¼ tsp)
  • Ginger40 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Miso paste40 g (2¼ tbsp)
  • Black sesame seeds6 g (2 tsp)
  • Sesame seeds6 g (2 tsp)
Rice
  • Medium grain rice190 g
  • Water380 ml (about 1½ cups)
  • Salt½ tsp
Veg
  • Fresh baby corn130 g
  • Baby pak choi4
  • Red radish75 g
  • Rice vinegar28 ml (about 2 tbsp)
Dressing
  • Lime1
  • Tahini40 g (2¾ tbsp)
  • Soy sauce13 ml (2½ tsp)
  • Agave syrup13 g (2 tsp)
  • Chilli flakes3 g (about 1¾ tsp)
To serve
  • Fresh coriander19 g

Method

  1. Bake eggplant. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Halve the eggplants lengthways, lay on a lined tray, drizzle with oil and bake 20–25 min until soft.
  2. Prep. Halve the baby corn and pak choi. Slice the radishes into rounds and mix with the rice vinegar and a pinch each of salt and brown sugar. Grate the ginger and garlic and mix with the miso, remaining brown sugar and 2½ tsp water: this is the glaze.
  3. Cook rice. Rinse the rice, add to a pot with the water and salt, bring to the boil, then cover and cook on low 10–12 min. Keep covered.
  4. Cook veg. Fry the pak choi and baby corn in oil over medium-high heat with a pinch of salt for 3–4 min. Add a splash of water, cover and cook 2 min more.
  5. Glaze. Brush the soft eggplants with the glaze on the cut side, sprinkle with both sesame seeds and bake 8–10 min more on high.
  6. Serve. Mix the tahini, soy sauce, agave and 2½ tsp water with a squeeze of lime and chilli flakes to taste. Serve the eggplant with the rice, veg and pickled radish, drizzled with the dressing and topped with coriander.

Air fryer: eggplant at 180°C for 12–15 min, then glazed for 6–8 min.

Tuesday 3 Nov

Cream of Mushroom Soup with Cheesy Panini

Soup30 minNeeds a blender

Ingredients

Soup
  • Chestnut mushrooms310 g
  • Shallots2½
  • Butter65 g
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Dried porcini mushrooms13 g
  • Garlic powder5 g (2 tsp)
  • Mushroom stock cube1
  • Water630 ml (about 2½ cups)
  • Soy sauce13 ml (2½ tsp)
  • Black pepper½ tsp
  • Cooking cream250 ml (about 1 cup)
Panini
  • Ciabatta1
  • Fresh chives19 g
  • Dijon mustard8 g (1½ tsp)
  • Grated cheddar75 g
  • Grated mozzarella75 g
  • Vegetable oil1¼ tbsp

Method

  1. Prep. Roughly chop the mushrooms. Finely slice the shallots.
  2. Sauté. Melt the butter in a pot over medium heat and fry the mushrooms with a pinch of salt for 5 min. Keep a couple aside for garnish. Add the shallots and dried porcini and fry 2 min.
  3. Simmer. Add the garlic powder, half the stock cube, the water, soy sauce, salt and pepper. Cover and simmer 20 min.
  4. Panini. Slice the ciabatta open. Mix the sliced chives with the mustard, cheddar and mozzarella and sandwich inside. Fry in a little oil over medium heat with a smaller pan weighed down on top, 3 min each side.
  5. Blitz. Blend the soup 2–3 min until smooth. Add the cream, heat through and season. Serve with the sliced panini.

A stick blender is fine.

Wednesday 4 Nov

Seabream with Creamy Chive Sauce, Tenderstem Broccoli and Green Peas

Fish20 minLow carb

Ingredients

Seabream
  • Seabream fillets410 g
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Vegetable oil1¼ tbsp
Greens
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Tenderstem broccoli190 g
  • Green peas190 g
Chive sauce
  • Fresh chives19 g
  • Butter13 g
  • Onion powder3 g (1¼ tsp)
  • Cooking cream250 ml (about 1 cup)
  • White balsamic vinegar19 ml (1¼ tbsp)
  • Salt½ tsp

Method

  1. Prep. Boil a kettle and pour the water into a large pot with a generous pinch of salt. Finely chop the chives.
  2. Boil veg. Add the tenderstem broccoli and cook over medium-high heat 5 min until tender. Add the peas for 1 min more. Drain.
  3. Fry fish. Pat the fillets dry and salt the skin. Fry skin-side down in a little oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat 4 min until crisp, flip and cook 1–2 min more. Move to a plate; drain the pan and keep it.
  4. Make sauce. Melt the butter in the pan over medium heat. Add the onion powder and stir 30 sec. Add the cream and chives and simmer 2 min until thick. Stir in the vinegar and season with salt.
  5. Serve. Serve the seabream with the chive sauce, broccoli and peas.

Friday 6 Nov

Orangey Chicken Thigh and Roasted Vegetables Traybake with Green Beans

Chicken40 min

Ingredients

Traybake
  • Skinless boneless chicken thighs500 g
  • Cauliflower500 g
  • Vegetable oil1¾ tbsp
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Red onion1½
  • Orange1
  • Smoked paprika2 g (¾ tsp)
  • Dried oregano2 g (about 1½ tsp)
  • Rice vinegar18 ml (1¼ tbsp)
  • Large zucchini1
Garlic aioli
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Mayonnaise65 g (4½ tbsp)
  • Lemon1
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp
Green beans
  • Green beans210 g
  • Salt½ tsp

Method

  1. Roast cauliflower. Preheat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Cut the cauliflower into florets, toss on a tray with oil and salt, and bake 5 min. Set aside.
  2. Marinate. Quarter the red onions. Zest the orange into a large bowl and mix with the smoked paprika, oregano, rice vinegar, a good pinch of salt and a drizzle of oil. Toss the chicken and onion in it.
  3. Roast. Chop the zucchini. Add the chicken, onion and zucchini to the cauliflower tray and bake 20–25 min until the chicken is cooked through.
  4. Aioli. Mince the garlic. Mix the mayonnaise with a squeeze of lemon, a pinch of garlic, salt and pepper.
  5. Green beans. Trim and cook in salted boiling water 3–4 min. Drain.
  6. Serve. Serve the traybake with the green beans and aioli.

Orange zest is strong: go easy (about 1¼ tbsp). Leave the raw garlic out of the mayo if it is too punchy.
