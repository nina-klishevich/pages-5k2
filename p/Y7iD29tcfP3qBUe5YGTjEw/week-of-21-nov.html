Saturday 21 Nov

Tagliatelle with pepper cream and sausage

PastaTime not givenPepper cream about 15 min

Moved up from Tuesday on Nina's request. Source is the creator's reel caption; not yet compared with the video.

Ingredients

Tagliatelle (2.5 servings)
  • Egg tagliatelle375 g
  • Yellow pepper⅔
  • Red pepper⅔
  • Sausage meat125 g
  • Dry white wineabout 1¼ small glasses
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano75 g
  • Fresh basilas needed
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Olive oil and saltas needed

Method

  1. Peppers. Clean and roughly cut the peppers. Cook in a pan with the garlic and a good drizzle of oil for about 15 min, covered, on medium-low heat.
  2. Pepper cream. Remove the garlic. With a stick blender, blend the peppers into a cream with half of the Parmigiano and the basil.
  3. Sausage. In the same pan, brown the sausage meat well. Deglaze with the white wine over high heat and let the alcohol evaporate.
  4. Finish. Add the pepper cream to the pan and drain the tagliatelle, nicely al dente, straight into it. Add the rest of the Parmigiano and toss well in the pan.
  5. Serve generously.

Sunday 22 Nov

Classic Braised Beef Bourguignon with Mashed Potatoes

Red meat55 min

Ingredients

Bourguignon
  • Steak strips440 g
  • Carrot1
  • Mushrooms310 g
  • White onion1
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Black pepper¼ tsp
  • Plain flour19 g (2½ tbsp)
  • Vegetable oil1¼ tbsp
  • Dried bay leaf1
  • Tomato paste40 g (2¾ tbsp)
  • Water440 ml (about 1¾ cups)
  • Balsamic vinegar19 ml (1¼ tbsp)
  • Brown sugar6 g (1¼ tsp, packed)
  • Beef stock cube1
  • Fresh thyme13 g
  • Fresh parsley19 g
Potato mash
  • Potatoes750 g
  • Salt1¼ tsp
  • Butter13 g
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Method

  1. Prep. Chop the carrots into small bite-sized pieces. Wipe and chop the mushrooms. Finely chop the garlic and half the onion.
  2. Prep beef. Pat the steak strips very dry, season with salt and pepper and toss in the flour.
  3. Fry beef. Heat a large pot over high heat with oil. Fry the beef in small batches for 2–3 min until deeply browned. Set aside and keep the pot.
  4. Make stew. Add more oil, the mushrooms, onion and carrots with a pinch of salt; fry 5 min until browned. Add the garlic, bay leaf and tomato paste; fry 1 min. Return the beef with the water, balsamic, brown sugar, stock cube and thyme (half or all, to taste). Simmer, covered, on medium for 30–35 min until tender.
  5. Make mash. Peel and chop the potatoes; boil in salted water for 20 min until soft. Drain, mash with butter, season.
  6. Serve. Stir chopped parsley into the stew and serve on the mash.

The pot must be really hot before the beef goes in, or it stews instead of browning. Add a splash of water if the stew reduces too fast.

Monday 23 Nov

Crispy Parmesan Chicken with Vegetables and Caper Mayo

Chicken30 min

Ingredients

Chicken
  • Chicken breast500 g
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp
  • Parmesan40 g
  • Egg1
  • Panko breadcrumbs25 g
  • Vegetable oil2½ tbsp
Vegetables
  • Green beans190 g
  • Small zucchini2½
  • Red pepper1
  • Olive oil1¼ tbsp
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Dried basil3 g (about 2¼ tsp)
Caper mayo
  • Capers25 g (about 2¾ tbsp)
  • Mayonnaise65 g (4½ tbsp)

Method

  1. Coat chicken. Pat the chicken dry and slice each breast in half horizontally into two thin escalopes. Season well. Finely grate the Parmesan and mix with the panko on a plate. Beat the egg in a bowl. Dip the chicken in egg, then coat in the Parmesan crumbs.
  2. Prep vegetables. Trim the green beans, cut the zucchini into half moons, deseed and chop the red pepper.
  3. Make caper mayo. Finely chop the capers and mix with the mayonnaise.
  4. Fry chicken. Fry in batches in a generous drizzle of oil over medium-high heat, 3–4 min each side, until golden and cooked through.
  5. Stir-fry vegetables. In another pan, fry the green beans and pepper with a pinch of salt for 3–4 min. Add a splash of water, then the zucchini and dried basil; fry 2 min more and season.
  6. Serve the vegetables topped with the crispy chicken and the caper mayo on the side.

Alternatively, bake the breaded chicken on a lined tray, drizzled with oil, at 200°C (180°C fan) for 12–15 min.

Wednesday 25 Nov

Salmon in Sesame Sauce with Warm Japanese Mushroom and Spinach Salad

Fish30 min

Ingredients

Salmon
  • Skinless salmon fillet440 g
  • Vegetable oil¾ tbsp
  • Salt¾ tsp
  • Black pepper¾ tsp
Salad
  • Spinach380 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Shimeji mushrooms250 g
  • Soy sauce25 ml (1¾ tbsp)
  • Sesame oil18 ml (1¼ tbsp)
  • Sesame seeds13 g (1½ tbsp)
Sauce
  • Tahini35 g (2¼ tbsp)
  • Miso paste25 g (1½ tbsp)
  • Honey13 g (2 tsp)
  • Rice vinegar18 ml (1¼ tbsp)
  • Water25 ml (1¾ tbsp)
Extra
  • Sushi ginger50 g

Method

  1. Prep. Boil a kettle. Trim the spinach stalks, put the leaves in a colander and pour the boiled water over to wilt them. When cool enough to handle, squeeze well and chop roughly. Peel and mince the garlic.
  2. Prep sauce. Whisk the tahini, miso, honey and rice vinegar, gradually adding the water until smooth. Set aside.
  3. Fry mushrooms. Trim the base of the shimeji and break them apart. Fry in a little oil in a hot pan for 4 min until starting to brown.
  4. Toss salad. Add the garlic for 30 sec. Take off the heat and add the soy sauce, sesame oil, sesame seeds and chopped spinach. Toss and move to a serving bowl.
  5. Fry salmon. Portion the salmon. Return the pan (no need to wash it) to high heat with a little oil and fry the salmon 3–4 min each side until cooked through. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Serve the salmon with the spinach salad and sushi ginger, drizzled with the sesame sauce.

Craving carbs? Serve with white rice.

Thursday 26 Nov

Minestrone Soup with Chicken

SoupAbout 45 minNeeds 1 mirepoix cube

Servings are not stated in the source; the recipe is marked Needs check. US units as written.

Ingredients

Soup
  • Cannellini beans, soaked or tinned1 cup
  • Chicken legs3
  • Zucchini1
  • Green beans1 handful
  • Crushed tomatoes1 quart (about 950 ml)
  • Chicken bone broth2 cups (about 480 ml)
  • Mirepoix cube (freezer)1
  • Olive oil, salt, pepper, fresh herbs to finish

Method

  1. Pan-fry the chicken legs in olive oil over medium-high heat until browned. Remove from the pan.
  2. Chop the vegetables into bite-sized pieces and sauté in the chicken fat with salt and pepper, about 3 min until slightly browned.
  3. Add the mirepoix cube, chicken legs, beans, tomatoes and broth; season.
  4. Cover and simmer 30 min, until slightly reduced and the chicken comes off the bone.
  5. Remove the bones, check the seasoning and finish with fresh herbs.

Friday 27 Nov

Aubergine and onion frittata

VegetarianTime not givenAbout 20 min on the hob

Status is Recipe added, not Tested. Needs about 300 ml frying oil. The recipe page has the method from the reel summary; the reel is linked there.

Ingredients

Frittata (2.5 servings)
  • Eggs4–5
  • Aubergines375 g
  • Medium onion1
  • Soft cheese65 g
  • Parmesan or Grana30 g
  • Basil leaves4
  • Oil for fryingabout 300 ml
  • Saltas needed
  • Black pepperoptional

Method

  1. Prep. Wash and dry the aubergines, trim the ends and cut into small pieces. Peel and thinly slice the onion.
  2. Fry. Heat the oil in a pan and fry the aubergine; add the onion after 10–12 min. When golden and tender, drain and let cool about 10 min.
  3. Egg mix. In a large bowl, beat the eggs with salt, the grated Parmesan or Grana, the cubed soft cheese and torn basil. Stir in the cooled aubergine and onion.
  4. Cook. Pour a little oil into a hot pan, add the mixture, cover and cook on low heat 15 min. Flip and cook without the lid 6–7 min until set and golden.
